How it works ↓

Under the hood

Four techniques, one field

a. Delta-time render loop

Every frame advances the simulation by the real elapsed time, not by frame count — motion is identical on a 60 Hz laptop and a 144 Hz display. Damping uses 1 − e−k·dt so cursor smoothing stays framerate-independent too.

b. GPU morphing

Each particle carries two positions in a BufferGeometry — where it is and where it is going. A vertex shader mixes them with a per-particle stagger, so shapes dissolve edge-first instead of sliding as one rigid block.

c. GSAP-driven uniforms

GSAP never touches the DOM here. It tweens a single uProgress uniform from 0 to 1 and the GPU does the rest — one interpolated number choreographs 100,000 particles.

d. Adaptive performance

The field detects coarse pointers and starts mobile devices at a lower density, caps devicePixelRatio at 2, and honours prefers-reduced-motion by disabling auto-cycle and ambient drift.
